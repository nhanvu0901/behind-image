# Public-domain story niches (2026-11-12)

How I measured: YouTube search pages (this month), channel /shorts tabs and the public RSS feed (the top-level `<published>` date is the channel's creation date), all fetched on 2026-11-12 = **MEASURED**. yt-dlp was not used.

## Scorecard

| Niche | DEMAND | SMALL-CHANNEL PROOF | COMPETITION | RISK | FIT | DEPTH | Viral mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strange / dark true history (paintings + pre-1930 photos) | 5 | 5 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | One startling real image plus a "wait, that really happened?" fact, finished in 6-36 s |
| Bible stories (Doré, Tissot, Old Masters) | 5 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | Faith identity and "is it a sin to…?" questions; viewers share them |
| Art history: the story behind a painting | 4 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 5 | "Look closer" detail reveal, or painting vs. photo |
| Mythology as Shorts (Greek, Norse, Egyptian, yokai) | 2 | 2 | 1 | 4 | 5 | 5 | Familiar god, unfamiliar twist; the feed is flooded with near-identical uploads |
| Mythology as long-form compilations | 5 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | "Every X explained in 25 min" binge |
| Folklore / fairy-tale origins | 2 | 1 | 3 | 5 | 5 | 4 | "The Disney version lied" |
| Space (NASA / ESA images) | 1 | 1 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | Awe and danger |
| True crime / mysteries over documents | 1 | 1 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 5 | Unsolved cliffhanger |
| Original stories with AI images | 4 | 2 | 1 | 1 | 2 | 5 | Melodrama payoff |

## Evidence (all MEASURED 2026-11-12 unless marked)

- **History.** In this month's "dark history #shorts" search, the top results had 3.41M, 2.27M and 1.22M views. Benchmark: @HistorybyMae (1.93M subscribers, created 2024-03) gets 197k to 2.1M per Short over paintings and photos.
  - Small-channel proof:
    - @TheArtBehind: created 2026-08-07, 3.56K subscribers, 116 videos. Its last 14 Shorts got 12k-292k views each. They are 6-second clips with the story in the description.
    - @Artesora: created 2026-08-30, 1.09K subscribers. Mostly 15k-43k per Short.
    - @HistorixCyma: created 2026-11-03, 76 subscribers, 7 videos. Best Shorts 79k and 17k.
- **Bible.** In this month's search for Bible Shorts under 4 minutes, the top results had 3.48M, 3.11M and 2.66M views.
  - @bibleminimovies: created 2026-10-08, 2.78K subscribers, 12 videos. One Short hit 533k, two hit 14k, the rest got 1-6k.
  - @Whywouldgod: created 2026-06-12, now 22.3K subscribers. Shorts at 490k, 480k and 172k.
  - AI "Bible movies" posted as long-form get 1.2-4.2M views.
- **Art.** @Legends_retold0_0: created 2026-01-24, 1.47K subscribers. Peaks of 23k-38k, but most Shorts get about 2-6k.
- **Mythology.** This month, most myth Shorts got 5 to 1.6k views. @MythologyNow-App (created 2026-08-14, 261 subscribers) gets about 1.2k per Short, with two outliers at 22k and 11k.
  - Long-form does much better: @Kelevins, 15 min, 1,046,009 views in 3 weeks; @Yorpcat, 25 min, 1,129,973; @MysteriesOfMythology, 1,533,336.
- **Folklore.** @TheGrimmCut (created 2026-10-19): best Short 4.8k.
- **Space and true crime.** This month's search results topped out at 1.9k and 1.1k views.

## Policy and rights

- **Monetization policy.** "Repetitious" content was renamed "inauthentic" on 2025-07-15 (REPORTED). In January 2026, YouTube terminated 16 AI-slop channels with 4.7B combined views (REPORTED, tubefilter).
  - On 2026-07-21, YouTube's Trust & Safety chief named three non-monetizable categories (REPORTED, netinfluencer): template/repetitive content, distressing content, and AI personas giving finance, legal or health advice.
  - Enforcement is channel-wide. Faceless creators are reported as collateral damage (REPORTED, TNW 2026-06-15).
  - I found no creator report of a YPP approval or rejection specific to these niches.
  - Original researched narration, with an image chosen and edited per beat, is the transformation the policy asks for.
- **AI labels.** Since May 2026, photorealistic AI is auto-labelled (REPORTED). AI "historical photos" draw backlash from historians and museums. **Don't use AI images for real history.**
- **Image licences.**
  - Met Open Access is CC0. NASA images are generally PD, but the logo/insignia are not.
  - ESA images are CC BY-SA IGO only where the file says so; the rest are non-commercial.
  - British Museum images are CC BY-NC-SA, so **not usable for monetized videos**.
  - Post-1930 illustrations are copyrighted. Artesora uses 1957 and 1961 magazine art.

## Money

- **RPM.** Shorts RPM is mostly $0.07-0.20 per 1,000 views, which is 3-14% of long-form. Education long-form RPM is $14.97-18.23 (REPORTED, AIR, 274 channels, Sep 2026). No reliable history, Bible or mythology Shorts RPM was found.
- **New Shorts threshold (decisive).** From 2027-02-01, earning from the Shorts pool needs **10M qualified Shorts views per trailing 90 days** (REPORTED, 9to5google and newsshooter, 2026-08-10). At our size, Shorts earn nothing after that date. **Long-form compilations are the revenue plan**, and all three picks compile well: "10 strangest royal deaths in 12 minutes", "the Book of Genesis in Doré engravings", "10 paintings with dark secrets".

## Verdict

**Best bets**
1. Strange true history over PD paintings and photos.
2. Bible stories over Doré/Tissot.
3. Art history, which we already have.

**Avoid**
- Mythology as Shorts. Keep it for long-form only.
- AI-illustrated original stories.
- True crime.
- Space.

**What winners look like.** Short (6-36 s for history). The first frame is the image. A caps-lock curiosity title. Formats: "painting vs. real photo", or "X vs. Y" comparisons. Our 45-60 s format is too long for this niche.

**What the engine lacks**
- More image sources: Wikimedia Commons, LoC, Smithsonian, Rijksmuseum, AIC, NGA.
- A **hard licence gate.** `art_pipeline/hunt.py` currently says "no license gate"; it must reject NC and unknown licences.
- A 15-35 s mode.
- A split-screen "vs" template.
- An event-based (not artwork-based) story scout.
- Automatic captions.
- A compiler that stitches 10 Shorts into a long-form video with an attribution list.
