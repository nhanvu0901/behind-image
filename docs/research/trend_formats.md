# Rising short-video formats, Nov 2026, and which ones our engine can ride

Research date 2026-11-12. Labels: **M** = MEASURED (I fetched the number: 4 yt-dlp flat YouTube searches on 2026-11-12, raw output in `niche_research/trend_data/s1-s4.json`; flat search returns no upload dates, so these are lifetime views); **R** = REPORTED (named source and date); **E** = ESTIMATED (my judgement). The "Fad or durable" column and section 3 are my judgement.

## Platform context: what changed in 2026 (all R)
- **Oct 1, 2026:** the Shorts feed now gives less reach to channels that "primarily aggregate or re-upload" other creators' videos. YouTube's Creator Liaison says these do **not** make a clip original: "narration that merely describes what's on screen", small technical edits, and template-based bulk changes ([ppc.land, 2026-10-02](https://ppc.land/re-uploaded-shorts-lose-reach-as-youtube-favours-original-clips/)).
- **Jul 13, 2026:** YouTube restated its inauthentic-content rule as three groups that cannot earn money. (1) generic or repetitive content, with "image slideshows" and templated storylines named outright; (2) unsatisfying content, including unrelated AI clips stitched together and realistic fake footage; (3) AI personas giving health, legal or financial advice ([Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/07/13/youtube-inauthentic-content-monetization-policy-update/)). In January, 16 AI-slop channels were terminated or wiped (35M subscribers, 4.7B views) ([OpusClip, 2026-04-27](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ai-video-news-april-2026)).
- **AI labels:** since May 2026 they are an overlay on Shorts and can be applied automatically. YouTube says the label alone does not change reach or monetization ([9to5Google, 2026-05-27](https://9to5google.com/2026/05/27/youtube-updating-ai-content-labels/)).
- **Feb 1, 2027:** joining YPP through Shorts will need 20M views in 90 days. To earn Shorts revenue in any month, a channel will need 10M views over the trailing 90 days ([ppc.land, 2026-08-16](https://ppc.land/youtube-vp-cites-a-few-cents-to-justify-10-million-shorts-view-floor/)). **E:** a channel at our size will earn nothing from the Shorts revenue pool. Shorts become a way to find viewers, not income.
- **Sep 23, 2026:** YouTube launched **Shorts Series**: seasons and episodes, custom thumbnails and autoplay in order ([Technobezz](https://www.technobezz.com/news/youtube-shorts-series-tv-comments-creators)).
- **Other platforms:** Instagram stopped recommending reposted content (Apr 30, 2026, [Tubefilter](https://tubefilter.com/2026/04/30/instagram-removes-algorithm-recommendations-repost-content-aggregator)). Facebook favors original creators and does accept AI content (Mar 2026, [Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/)). Sora's app shut down on 2026-04-26 and its API on 2026-09-24 ([OpusClip](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ai-video-news-april-2026)). Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0 and Seedance 2.0 are now the main AI video tools.

## 2. Fading or punished formats: avoid these
- **Re-upload and clip channels.** Reach cut from Oct 1, 2026. **Risk for us:** clip-built "screen Q&A" where the narration only describes the footage. Every clip beat needs a claim or an analysis point that the footage alone does not show.
- **Image slideshows and templated series.** Named as non-monetizable on 2026-07-13. Our pan/zoom still-image format is close to this. Vary the structure, the hook and the source mix between videos.
- **Fake AI trailers and "leaked scene" videos.** Screen Culture and KH Studio were demonetized in May 2025 and terminated in December 2025 ([Film Stories](https://filmstories.co.uk/news/youtube-demonetises-fake-ai-generated-film-trailers/)). Doomsday fan scenes (format 7) belong to the same risk group.
- **Stitched AI clips without a story, and realistic fake events.** Non-monetizable under the July 2026 rules.
- **AI fruit and brainrot dramas.** TikTok removed videos. Re-uploads got little reach on YouTube (M), and viewers turned on them: the reaction videos get 1-3M views.
- **AI history POV.** Saturated (format 6).
- **Unlabeled AI on TikTok.** Reported penalty: about a 60% reach cut for 30 days after 3 unlabeled AI uploads (R, single vendor source; OpusClip).
- **Reddit-story + gameplay split screens and "AI faceless automation" channels.** Reported as hit by demonetization (R, weak sources: [THR 2026-06-13](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/digital/faceless-creators-youtube-ai-damage-1236617586/), vendor blogs).

## 3. Shortlist: 5 trends our engine could join within one week

| Rank | Trend | The concrete video | What we need to add |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-release primer (format 1) + comic vs screen | A 5-episode Shorts Series, "Doomsday in the comics", posted Dec 1-17. Ep1: who Doctor Doom is in the comics. Ep2: Secret Wars (2015) in 60 seconds. Ep3: what Incursions are. Ep4: Doom vs the Fantastic Four. Ep5: 3 comic panels the film may recreate. | Group the episodes in a Shorts Series; a "Part X/5" end card. The comic source and panels are what make it original. |
| 2 | Episodic microdrama (format 3) applied to comic arcs | Split a multi-issue arc into 4-6 Shorts of 45 seconds, each ending on a cliffhanger, published as a Series. | A splitter in Stage 1 (beat → episode boundary); a cliffhanger rule. |
| 3 | Lore explainer (format 2) for the films opening now | "Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping — who is Haymitch?", explained from the books (Nov 18-22); Godzilla lore (in theaters since Nov 6). | A new source adapter: book or wiki facts instead of comic panels. Stills would come from official press kits (fair-use risk is moderate). |
| 4 | Game-launch lore (format 8): a test on a side channel | "The real Florida stories behind GTA 6's missions", 45 seconds, posted in the week of Nov 19. | A clip adapter that uses official trailers (Content ID risk: medium). Fit with our comic audience is low, so test it on a separate channel. |
| 5 | Reacting to AI slop (format 5), done with paintings | Through the art pipeline: "AI made the Titanic. Here is what the real 1912 paintings and photos show", using public-domain museum images versus AI frames. | A side-by-side layout; a "myth vs record" script prompt. No face, so the evidence gap is large. |

**E:** #1 and #2 have the best expected value. They extend what we already do, the risk is low, and Shorts Series rewards a series that is watched in order. AI video tools add only cost and policy risk for us. If we use them, it should be one or two establishing shots, labeled, and never a whole video.
