# Same videos, other languages and platforms (research 2026-11-12)

## Key evidence
**Demand and competition by language (MEASURED with yt-dlp on the /shorts tab, 2026-11-12):**
- ES: *The Top Comics* (MX), 4.78M subs. Median of its last 30 Shorts is 236k, max 653k. Most are MCU/news, not comic narration. youtube.com/channel/UCiBwOVBljEjcsLUjwI274Xw/shorts
- PT-BR: *HQnow* (@hqnow), 4.17k subs. It makes the same format as ours (Marvel/DC comic narration). Median of its last 10 Shorts is 1.75k, median of 30 is 8.1k, max 107k. **It is stuck at the same level as our English channel.** This is the closest small-channel data point, and it does not count as proof.
- VI: *Tạp Hóa Comic*, 73.8k subs, Marvel/DC trivia Shorts. Median of its last 10 is 6.5k, median of 30 is 22k, max 828k. *Comik Nerd*, another VI Marvel/DC channel, has no Shorts tab at all, so there are few Vietnamese comic Shorts makers. VI manhwa recaps are long-form and declining: *Ngọc Hương Review* has 150k subs but only 91k views in 28 days (REPORTED, vidIQ, 2026-11-10, vidiq.com/youtube-stats/channel/UCigHUUdcHOBYTOV7YVw5qUQ/).
- HI: *Superhero Comics*, 389k subs, has only 2 Shorts (38k and 85k), so it is inactive on Shorts.
- I found no small channel started in 2026 that gets 10k+ views per Short in any non-English comic niche. No ID, AR, DE or FR comic Shorts channels turned up in searches.

**Shorts RPM per 1,000 views** (REPORTED, fluxnote.io/guides/youtube-shorts-rpm-by-country, 2026-06-26, estimates): US $0.10-0.32, DE $0.09-0.16, FR $0.06-0.10, BR $0.03-0.05, MX $0.03-0.04, IN $0.01-0.02, ID $0.01-0.02. For VN, one music publisher reports about $0.18-0.22 across all YouTube formats (not Shorts-only), with months as low as $0.018 (REPORTED, dynamoi.com/data/youtube-adsense-rpm/vn, Nov 2025-Oct 2026).

**Vietnam audience size** (REPORTED, DataReportal Digital 2026 Vietnam, Oct 2025 data): ad reach is Facebook 79.0M, TikTok 76.1M (18+), YouTube 62.1M, Instagram 11.7M.

## Voice
- **Chatterbox Multilingual v3** (MIT, open weights, released 2026-06-10) supports 25 languages, including ES with es-mx/es-es packs, PT with a pt-br pack, HI (pack), FR, DE, AR and VI. Resemble itself **advises against using it for Vietnamese (75% CER)**. It has **no Indonesian**. Resemble retired Chatterbox commercially in Aug 2026, so there will be no further updates (REPORTED, resemble.ai/resources/chatterbox-multilingual-v3-tts-with-embedded-watermarking-for-25-languages).
- **Vietnamese alternative: VieNeu-TTS**. It runs locally, clones a voice from a 3-5 s sample, and was trained on 1,000 h of data. The 0.5B model is Apache-2.0; the 0.3B model is CC BY-NC (REPORTED, pypi.org/project/vieneu/). Microsoft Edge neural voices are free online and also cover ID.
- Vietnamese movie-recap creators already use AI voices widely (FPT.AI, Viettel AI), so a synthetic voice is normal there. I found no survey on audience acceptance (REPORTED, fptshop.com.vn/tin-tuc/giai-tri/giong-review-phim-206814).

## Platforms
- **YouTube Shorts:** YPP works in VN. The YouTube policy is "inauthentic content" (renamed 2025-07-15). It targets mass-produced output, and our re-voiced narration counts as transformative.
- **TikTok Creator Rewards:** pays only for videos **over 1 minute**, needs 10k followers, and is limited to about 8 countries (US/UK/DE/FR/BR/MX/JP/KR, or a similar list). **VN, ID and IN are not on any list** (REPORTED, ttcalculator.net/learn/creator-fund-countries/, 2026-07-09; sources conflict).
- **Facebook:** the unified Content Monetization programme is still limited or invite-only in VN. Reels have no fixed public threshold. Reposts and TikTok logos lead to restriction (REPORTED, tienphong.vn 2026-07-27). Meta's July 2025 rules cut distribution and monetisation for repeat reposters, but transformed content is exempt.
- **Cross-posting:** no platform documents a penalty for uploading the same clean master file to several platforms. **Watermarks** (TikTok or YouTube logos) are what get videos demoted.

## Dubbing
YouTube auto-dub opened to all creators in Feb 2026. It dubs EN into 20 languages (ES, PT, HI, ID, FR, DE and AR among them) but **not into VI**. A Content ID claim blocks dubbing (REPORTED, support.google.com/youtube/answer/15569972). Multi-language audio tracks work on Shorts, but **no source confirms that auto-dub runs on Shorts**, and I found no data on Shorts views from dubbed tracks (REPORTED, videodubbing.com 2026-08-01).

## Answer
**Best 3:**
1. **VI on YouTube Shorts plus the same file on the owner's Facebook pages.** There are few Vietnamese comic Shorts makers, and the owner can judge script and voice quality himself.
2. **EN auto-dub plus clean cross-posting to TikTok, Reels and Facebook.** It costs almost nothing.
3. **ES (LatAm) on Shorts.** It has the biggest demand and the best Chatterbox fit, but it is saturated and the owner cannot check the Spanish.

**Avoid:**
- ID: no voice and low RPM.
- HI: RPM about $0.01.
- PT-BR: measured results are no better than ours.
- Expecting TikTok income from a VN account.
- Watermarked reposts.
- Chatterbox for Vietnamese.

**Two-week experiment:**
- Take our 10 best-performing English Shorts, whose scripts are already proven.
- Translate their narration.json with the LLM.
- Voice them with VieNeu-TTS 0.5B and reuse the existing shots.
- Post 1 per day for 10 days to a new VI YouTube channel, and the same clean file to the owner's Facebook page.
- At the same time, check the Studio "Languages" tab on the EN Shorts to see whether auto-dub tracks appear.
- **Success:** VI Shorts median of 5k+ views at 7 days (3x our EN 1.6k median), or any Short reaching 20k+; FB Reels median of 10k+ plays.
- **Failure:** a median under 2k. In that case, stop.
