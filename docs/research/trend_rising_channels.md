## 4. Study first
1. **GreenBroRants** (and Gneef/MollySpell): less than 13 months old, +1.12M subs in 28 days, MEASURED median 2.3M. The one-line "what if everyone…" premise maps onto our comic What-If formula.
2. **His Story**: narrated first-person stories with a revenge payoff, at about 1 per day; MEASURED median 697k. Closest in narrative structure to our recaps.
3. **AuraShorts**: created Nov 2025; "Ranking X Moments" is a fill-in-the-blank series, MEASURED median 2.75M. It fits our clip mode (ranking comic or film moments).
4. **Thế Giới Có Gì Hay?**: VN narration over curiosity clips, MEASURED median 468k. This is the format nearest to our engine, in a market we speak.
5. **Zack D. Films**: the archetype the risers in group 2 copy. It went from #12 Global (Nov 2025) to #1 (Oct 2026).

## 5. Patterns most risers share
1. **One fixed title template per channel, used as a series**: "Ranking ___ Moments", "Everyone Has ___", "___ the SOUND", "Part N". Each new video is a variation, not a new idea.
2. **Little or no language dependence**: titles of 2 to 6 words plus an emoji, faceless, and many labeled "Remix". The channels set a US country while being made elsewhere (PK, IN, VN, EG).
3. **Burst growth from a few 20-110M hits within one month** on new or pivoted channels. Uploads are often several per day. The algorithm rewards a format that hits, not channel age.
