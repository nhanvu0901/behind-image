# Bottom-up outliers: what broke out in 2026 (faceless, narrated stills/clips)

Date: 2026-11-12. Agent scope: evidence-first, any topic.

## Method
Web reports (vidIQ, OverseerOS, OutlierKit, Kapwing, Tubefilter, Reddit via search) named **no** specific new faceless channels. Every vendor "case study" was anonymous. So I built the list myself: 56 YouTube Shorts topic searches (Shorts filter) gave 1,391 Shorts. I kept 671 with 100k+ views and resolved their 549 channels (oEmbed + `/about` page: join date, subs, total views, video count). **92 of the 549 joined on or after 2025-09-01.** Below are 20 that are faceless-narrated by title and description (not watched), across 7 topics. I excluded anime/music edits, 3D/Blender what-ifs, AI-video vlogs and text-only ranking compilations. All `/about` numbers are MEASURED on 2026-11-12 at `https://www.youtube.com/@<handle>/about`. "Median" means the yt-dlp flat listing of the last ≤30 Shorts (MEASURED, 6 calls used). "Avg/video" is total views ÷ videos (ESTIMATED: one hit inflates it).

Viral mechanisms, in the same order:
1. A famous image plus "you never noticed this". The answer sits inside the frame you are already looking at.
2. Real old photo = proof. Each new photo is a fresh mini-hook.
3. A known name plus a twist ("Why Athena threw the flute away").
4. Insider detail on something the viewer already loves.
5. Rapid novelty, every 5-10 s.
6. A famous person's arc in under a minute.
7. Dread plus "true".

## Format mechanics (n = 20; counted from titles)
- **Reveal framing** ("the truth/secret/real… behind"): 11/20.
- **One real image as the evidence** (painting/archival photo on screen): 9/20.
- **Numbered 3-5 item list**: 6/20.
- **Fixed series template** (same title or structure every upload): 5/20. WU30 gets a 37k median with one identical title.
- **Narrator persona in the channel name** (Arthur, Lina, Eira, Theo, "I'm Alex"): 5/20.
- **Claimed very short (≤30 s)**: 2/20. The others were not measured.
- **"Part 2" hooks**: 0/20. They appear only in excluded ranking, edit and royal-gossip channels (7 of the 92).
- **Comment-bait endings**: not measurable without watching.

## Recommendation
**Niches:**
1. Art "story behind the image". The sister pipeline already uses public-domain museum images.
2. Mythology told through public-domain paintings.
3. Single-era dark history from archival photos (the civilwarfineprint model).

**Mechanics to copy first:**
1. Reveal framing anchored on one famous image.
2. One fixed per-series title/structure template.
3. 3-5 item lists for the photo-carousel variant.

## Bias and unverified claims
- **Survivorship.** The sample came from search results for 100k+ Shorts. Base rates are far lower. vidIQ (2026-07-28, REPORTED, https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-first-10-videos-study/) puts the median 30-day Short at about 1.3k views for new channels. OverseerOS (2026-10-31, REPORTED, https://www.overseeros.com/blog/can-youtube-shorts-go-viral-with-under-1000-subscribers) found only 5.3% of Shorts from channels under 1k subs reach 10k. Our 1.6k median is roughly normal.
- **Averages overstate.** DHUSA09 averages 46k but its median is 3.4k (13×). Treat every "avg" as an upper bound.
- **Too early.** civilwarfineprint (4 Shorts) and artloris (2 months old) are early.
- **Not confirmed by watching.** Faceless status, voice type (AI or human) and length were not checked. Lina, Arthur and Damian-style personas may use a real voice.
- **Image rights.** artloris and Arthur also use modern photos and contemporary art, which carry copyright and claim risk. Public-domain-only use is safer.
- **Anomalies.** @CreaturVaultOfficial (484K subs, 4 videos) and @VeilixVox (75K, 2 videos) look like anomalies and were excluded.
