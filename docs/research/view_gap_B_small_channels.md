# B_report: do small comic-Shorts channels break out? (benchmark agent, 2026-11-12)

**Method.** I found channels through YouTube video search (mostly fan edits, little use) and channel-type search (gives subscriber counts: 143 niche candidates, 100 to 60k subs). I checked activity with each channel's public RSS feed (its 15 newest uploads with dates and views), then took flat /shorts listings for 58 channels and full metadata for 341 videos (the newest 12 per channel for 27 channels). "Active" means a Short posted on or after Oct 1, 2026. "Aged" means at least 4 days old. Flat-listing counts are rounded. No request was blocked. **Bias:** search ranks channels that already had some traction, so this sample probably overstates how well small channels do.

## The size question

**Answer (medium confidence):** Most of the gap is not size, but our view level is normal for a channel of our size **in today's niche**. Among 16 active channels under 2k subs (us included), 13 have aged medians of 0.03–2.7k. We are at the middle of that group (1,541 vs a group median of about 1,700). Only 3 clearly break out: MARVEL-ousUniverse (283 subs, 23.6k), VoidComics-8x (970 subs, 5.2k) and Comiclore_28 (758 subs, 4.8k).

**Two groups, but split by era more than by skill (measured).**
- **Stuck near 1–2.7k:** 10 active channels (plus 3 under 400 views and 1 near-zero).
- **At 6–28k:** 7 channels under 2k subs: TheComicsHub 16.8k, THWIP 28k, Comicsv12 15.4k, Comic_Shorts04 10.3k, WhatIfComics 10.0k, ComicXplained 8.8k, ComicCut-77 6.1k. **All 7 are dormant.** Their last uploads were between Aug 2024 and Mar 2026.
- **The July small channels have also stopped:** ComicCut-77 (last upload Mar 9, 2026) and VillainHubs (Jul 2) are dormant.
- Only 2 active channels under 2k subs sit at 5k, and 1 sits above 20k.
- Inferred: medians of 6–12k for under-1k-sub channels were common in 2024 and early 2026. That level is rare now, which matches D_report's finding that big-channel medians have fallen.

**Size vs views (measured, n=17 active channels under 10k subs, from RSS):** Spearman ρ = 0.49. Larger channels tend to do a bit better, but there is wide scatter. A 283-sub channel gets 24k, and a 5.8k-sub channel gets 1.3k. Our views per subscriber (23) is already among the highest.

**Do channels under 1k subs reach 10k+?** Only MARVEL-ous does so routinely (7/7). Comiclore reached 10k+ on 3 of 10, and BlindComics on 2 of 12. Everyone else did on 0. Rare viral hits do happen (Comiclore 572k, Void 631k, comicversetvu 1.3M), but they are one-offs.

## What the breakout small channels do that we don't

1. **Fill the whole vertical frame (inferred from frames).** Comiclore and Void show full-screen 9:16 comic art that changes about every 3 s. Comiclore stacks real comic panels with one short caption line and no title card. Void uses glossy character art with word-by-word captions in yellow and white. July forensics found our videos with a blurred top half and a small panel, so check whether our current renders still do this.
2. **Retell one specific comic and name its source (measured).** Every Comiclore description starts "Comic book: <title> – <issue>". Its top videos are Doctor Strange/Hulk "Best Defense" (19.8k, 11.3k) and Dark Crisis (16.2k). Void writes a one-sentence hook as its description. Both use tags (92–100%). Ours have no description and no tags. Stuck channels vary on this, so it is weak evidence.
3. **Ride current movie demand (measured for one channel).** MARVEL-ous posts 10–15 s MCU film clips, letterboxed, with a two-line red and white title ("IRON MAN'S NANOSUIT WAS GREAT BUT…"), keyword captions and "KINDLY SUBSCRIBE". It posts 7.6 a week, all tied to Avengers: Doomsday and Brand New Day. It runs on clips rather than comic panels, so it is not our format.
4. **Shorter titles (weak).** 5.7–7.3 words vs our 8.3. Stuck channels also use 6.5–8.7 words.
5. **Not different:** duration (14–83 s at the breakouts, no pattern), cadence (2.0–2.3/wk at Comiclore and Void, below ours), like/view (1.6–3.0% vs our 2.7%), "Part 1/2" series (none), and The Boys/Invincible (5 titles across 993 small-channel titles, median 0.69× the channel median).

## What the data cannot tell
- Retention, swipe rate and traffic sources, which decide the first test batch.
- Whether the decline after early 2026 comes from the platform or from these channels.
- Causality behind any of the differences above. Three breakout channels is anecdote, not proof.
- Whether art in Void is AI-generated. It looks uniform, but frames at 144p can't confirm it.
- Voices: I did not judge them.
